Data-Efficient Humanoid Loco-Manipulation Learning via Heterogeneous Human Data Pretraining and Iterative Offline RL

Abstract

Learning whole-body loco-manipulation from teleoperated robot data is costly and hard to scale. Human data offers a far cheaper and more diverse option for robot skill learning: it is already available at scale and used successfully for table-top bimanual robot arms. Transferring the trained policy's success to humanoids is harder: the visual and kinematic gap is wider than table top bimanual arms. Mmost human data capture head and both wrists' trajectory but not the whole-body motion that the low level tracker for humanoid takes in. Those data are also stored in different formats, and different datasets have very different observation spaces and action spaces. In this work, we propose a new policy training recipe for learning humanoid whole-body loco-manipulation skills, in which we pretrain our policy by co-training on a large amount of human data that are canonicalized into the same action space, and post-train the policy with human-in-the-loop iterative offline RL. Our key insight is that a large amount of in the wild human data provides distributional coverage for the policy to learn how to accomplish the whole-body manipulation task in different scenarios, and a small amount of on-robot human-in-the-loop post-training shapes and sharpens that distribution to achieve a higher success rate in accomplishing hard loco-manipulation tasks in the wild. We demonstrate how our recipe performs in selected whole-body manipulation tasks that span the demands of whole body loco-manipulation: our trained policy reaches a 20/20 success rate in the any-object pickup task with language conditioning and whole body posture coordination, measured in unseen environments throughout. Our policy also achieves 17/20 and 16/20 success rates on pushing and pulling doors with heavy door knobs. The recipe works effectively in tasks involving whole-body contact like torso-assisted whole-body box carrying, and achieves a 16/20 success rate in held-out environments. Additional training recipe's introduction and experimental results are available at https://wbm-with-human-data-and-rl.github.io/.
